## Your agents

You decide what each agent may do: its allowed actions, its caps, its co-sign line and how long it lasts. The contract enforces those limits, so an agent can't move more than its remaining caps. Within them, an agent does what it is told, and you are responsible for the agents you connect and the permissions you give them.

The guardian and AI review add caution, but they can be wrong in either direction: they can hold or reject an operation you wanted, or let through one you didn't, inside your limits. They are not a promise that every operation is safe. Keep each agent's session key secret, and stop an agent you no longer trust.
